# Bildbriefar

## Stillgrottan, omslagsbild

Genererad med det inbyggda Imagegen-verktyget för Auralunds första gestaltning. Bilden används som originalkonst i världsboken och Vävarboken. Transparent bakgrund: nej.

Färger, drakarnas anatomi, växter, konkreta klippformer och belysning är fria konstnärliga val. Den bevarade kärnan är den stora skuggiga grottan och den mycket stora genomskinliga bubbellika kristallen, utan inflyttade möbler. Omslaget anger ingen ny korridor eller grottplan.

### Fullständig bildprompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: portrait cover artwork for an original fantasy world bible called Auralund; no text in image. Primary request: a deeply warm, contemplative, richly painted fantasy book cover, full portrait composition, about ROOT, STAR and TONE. This is newly authorized imaginative world design, NOT a reconstruction of any historical tradition or the pages of a real book. Scene: inside an enormous shadowy cave, the walls deep blue green stone with subtle natural mineral veining. A very large entirely transparent bubble-like crystal floats visually in the spacious central area, its edges faintly opalescent sea green, the interior EMPTY, no furniture or thrones. To one side, the original female seer dragon Selvarin rests calmly, graceful dragon shape with translucent opalescent crystal scales and a quiet compassionate gaze, tasteful new creature design, not a human. In lower foreground a much smaller friendly original book dragon Eiravel with indigo scales, copper wings and warm amber eyes holds an open worn book close to his paws. A few delicate roots and fern silhouettes in foreground frame the view; no doorway through these plants. A small warm amber light by the book provides intimate human scale. Hints of star light reflected in the mineral veins, thin copper arcs like an instrument's vibration, but no literal music notation. Organic druidic woodland sensitivity meets the material restraint of ancient stone, lapis, copper and warm gold; no copied religious symbols, no pyramids, no deity statues. Style: masterful painterly fantasy cover with fine detail and real tactile stone, elegant quiet spaciousness, NOT cute cartoon, NOT glossy video game poster. Palette: deep pine green, midnight indigo, grey jade, amber and aged copper. Top 20 percent dark uncluttered space for title to be added later. Mood of friendship, listening and wonder. No people, no crowd, no text, no logo, no watermark, no map or diagram. Keep bubble clearly transparent and EMPTY; do not add seated figures or furniture within it. Do not show a garden connected by a corridor; no additional rooms, portals or inferred floor plan.

### Resultat

Projektfil: `game/assets/auralund-stillgrottan.png`. Ingen befintlig bild ersattes. Inga ändringar av ett existerande apphusdjur gjordes.

## Glödtonens kammare

Genererad med det inbyggda Imagegen-verktyget som egen illustration av projektets musikrum. Gestaltens utseende, instrument och de konkreta ytornas form är nya val. Rummet kopplas inte till någon annan grotta i bilden eller kartan.

### Fullständig bildprompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide interior illustration for Auralund's original world bible. Primary request: an intimate, contemplative music cave, called Glödtonens kammare, with no printed text. A standing viewer's first-person eye-level perspective inside a single very smooth domed cave, near a tiny fire approximately two metres ahead. Next to the tiny fire is a raised round stone platform; on it is Glim, a VERY SMALL fully clothed elderly fantasy musician and listener, only about 25 centimetres tall, wearing a simple dark moss-green robe and copper-brown cloak and holding a tiny acoustic stringed instrument. He must occupy only a small part of the frame. He is an original warm, quietly wise story character, no identifiable real person. The cave dome is smooth and quiet, dark gold, red-brown, orange fire light and copper; no jagged stalactites, no glittering crystals, no furniture, no crowds, no other characters, no dragons. Several small musical notes are engraved in the round platform stone: most glow extremely faintly; just ONE glows a little more strongly, a small visual answer to the sound. Keep this musical notation subtle, not floating. Entire scene intimate and inward-looking, low density, restrained light, plentiful real darkness, fine atmospheric painted texture, luxurious literary fantasy illustration rather than a videogame spectacle. This is the project's own original music room, not a claimed depiction of a Conway-book location. The room has no depicted door or corridor and is NOT connected in the image to any other cave or garden. All clothes modest and complete. No words, no watermark, no logo, no decorative golden borders. Landscape composition, 3:2 or 16:9.

### Resultat

Projektfil: `game/assets/auralund-glodtonens-kammare.png`. Ingen befintlig bild ersattes.
