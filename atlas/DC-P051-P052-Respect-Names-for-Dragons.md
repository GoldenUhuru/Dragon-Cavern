## Etiquette (p. 51)
- Do not call a dragon by its personal name without direct permission. Calling a dragon by only its clan name is also rude.
- Female dragons can hold any position a male dragon can and deserve equal regard.
- Appearance, coloring, size and manner of speaking do not distinguish female from male dragons.
- These titles are intended for respectful communication with dragons.
