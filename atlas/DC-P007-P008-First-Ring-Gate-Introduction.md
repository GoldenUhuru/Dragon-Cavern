## Ordered scene evidence

### Page 7
1. Student seems to move extremely fast, yet feels motionless.
2. A **huge wooden door** with **iron scrollwork and hinges** appears as **velvety darkness** turns into **bright sunlight**.
3. Student pulls a **heavy chain attached to a bell**; its deep sound reverberates repeatedly; heavy door slowly opens.
4. A **brightly coloured dragon just inside door** asks why visitor has come.
5. Student says they wish to **study within the Five Inner Rings**. Dragon beckons student inside; **door closes behind**.
6. Student senses **circular shape of First Ring**; visually sees **spacious but narrow room, with slightly curved walls**. **Sunlight streams through skylights** but student **cannot see ceiling**.

### Page 8
7. To right and left: **endless lines of tables** holding different objects; **shelves along walls** crowded with **scrolls and books**; **diagrams and maps on walls**; **open cabinets**, **jars of dried herbs**, **tall bottles of oils**.
8. Gate dragon asks whether student wishes to study with dragons and instructs them, **if certain**, to **sign registry**.
9. **Large clawed foot** points to table next to **closed door**; **quill pen standing in ink bottle**, **open book**.
10. Student signs **their name beneath others' names** in book when sincere.
11. Gate guardian attaches **tiny silver dragon figure** to student's **clothing**.
12. Student may ask Gate dragon **one question**.
13. Guardian leads student to **brilliant cone of light** reaching **endlessly upward from stone floor**.
14. Gate dragon says when visitor returns they should identify as **student of Five Inner Rings**; expresses hope of seeing them soon.
15. Gate dragon's **clawed hand** points at light cone; student approaches light.

The next printed marker is **“(Ending)”**; the student walks into light and returns to physical awareness. That return is documented separately in the standardized ending entry `DC-P007-P008-Standard-Meditation-Opening-Ending.md`.
