# DC-P060 — Basic Personal Protection

**Source:** D. J. Conway, *Mystical Dragon Magick*, pp.60–61. **Evidence:** Directly readable frames in uploaded p42–66 video, approx. 00:30–00:31. **Status: VERIFIED source reading, USER-APPROVED 2026-10-24.**

**p60:** Conway claims few people encounter authentic magical attack, and attributes many perceived troubles to envy and ill-wishing. She recommends (1) visualization of a globe of **brilliant white light**; (2) if remaining near a perceived aggressor, visualization of **shining blue armor**; (3) wearing a meaningful **consecrated good-luck charm** (examples include a crystal alone or held by a dragon figure). The charm consecration described involves frankincense smoke, heat above candle flame, water and contact with earth as four elements. She also advises practical avoidance of predictable conflict and staying observant.

**p61:** **Look Elsewhere** technique: if unable to avoid conflict, use one earlier protection visualization, avoid eye contact, move slowly and smoothly along intended path, and mentally direct attention away from oneself.

**Boundaries:** These are Conway's magical beliefs and suggestions, not scientifically verified protective effects. The flame/heat element carries a physical burn risk; do not present it as a safety recommendation. No specific dragon, Otherworld site or architectural setting is named.

### P.60 — author claims and three techniques
- Conway differentiates what she calls rare authentic magical attacks from more common ill-wishing, jealousy and envy (especially by non-magicians), claiming an accumulation of negative vibrations in the house or aura can manifest as clumsiness, small accidents, errors, misunderstandings, and time/money loss. **These are attributed claims, not evidence-backed physics or psychology.**
- **1. White-light globe:** immediately visualize body totally surrounded by brilliant white light, enclosed in a globe; alleged purpose to block negativity and give time to distance oneself from an aggressor.
- **2. Blue armor:** if remaining near the perceived aggressor, visualize shining blue armor covering every part of the body, portable without constant concentration.
- **3. Consecrated charm:** meaningful good-luck charm; example a crystal, sometimes held by a **dragon's foot** or by an **entire dragon figure**; crystal alone also named. Conway describes successive contact with frankincense incense smoke, the upper heat of a candle flame, sprinkled water, and touch to earth or a bowl of earth, naming the four elements. This records her text, **not a safety recommendation to handle candle flames**.
- **Practical priority:** Conway says the best protection is to avoid a known troublemaker or foreseeable conflicts; remain observant.

### P.61 — conclusion
- If avoidance impossible, use one of preceding protection methods **plus a "Look Elsewhere" spell**.
- **No eye contact** with anyone.
- **Slow, smooth movement** along necessary path.
- **Mentally focus on a direction away from self**, strongly imagining that something interesting is there, to direct attention elsewhere.
- The section ends **before** the separately headed **Dragon Ride Meditation** on p.61.

### Source-visual limits and distinctions
- No Otherworld location, named dragon participant or precise charm design mandated; a crystal held by dragon foot / complete dragon figurine are stated **options**, not a single required shape.
- The white light is a **globe surrounding** the practitioner; blue armor **covers** body. Do not combine into one object.
- Statements about attacks, vibration, aura and protection remain Conway's spiritual model, **not independently verified efficacy**.
