# DC-P061 — Dragon Ride Meditation

**Primary evidence:** Conway, *Mystical Dragon Magick*, pp.61–62, directly read from supplied video around 00:31–00:32. **Status: VERIFIED from readable page frames; user approval PENDING.** A scripted guided visualization, not a literal travel account.

## Page 61
- Standard meditation opening **p.7**.
- At rosy-pink dawn, student walks through **stone-paved courtyard of an ancient castle**; climbs stone stairs built into **outer wall**.
- Co-magician dragon awaits with wings slightly spread; **green and gold scales** glisten in sunlight.
- Dragon bends a knee to help student grasp **harness strap** and sit on **shoulders between wings**.
- Dragon offers a flight; **invisible warm-air bubble** surrounds both as protection from cold/airless skies and a fall.
- Student grips harness; dragon launches from castle wall on thermal air currents.
- Visible landscape: patchwork fields, dirt road edged by trees, shrub-lined canal fed from river beyond castle.
- Dragon banks **left** and flies **southeast** across western United States: Pacific Northwest, high desert plateaus, Arizona/New Mexico, red rock, cliff dwellings, Grand Canyon.

## Page 62
- Student requests **Mississippi River, Atlantic Ocean, ancient places in the Far East**.
- Dragon offers journey on condition of answering **one question at end**; correct answer permits progression to next Ring; failure referred to **Council of Elders**, who decide repeat Apprentice studies or continue only mundane levels.
- Follows Mississippi from **Gulf of Mexico to Great Lakes**; student sees imagined Earth's energy lines and glowing volcanic sites when looking with 'inner psychic eyes' (author's visualization, not measured physical phenomena).
- Visits **Great Wall of China**; dragon shields riders from human sight. Other destinations are open to student choice.
- Return to castle. Question: what tools are needed to have power to cast a spell? **Answer stated at top of p.63: power comes from within; tools do not have power of themselves.**
- Lighted door opens in castle wall; standard meditation ending **p.8**.

Castle outer wall here must not be uncritically equated with **wall-less courtyard** in p.58; these are two descriptions in distinct meditations. Flight path and compass direction stated on p.61.
