# DC-P061 — Dragon Ride Meditation

**Primary source:** D. J. Conway, *Mystical Dragon Magick*, printed pp.61–63. Original supplied video: `CH1 P42-66 _ The Way Of The DRAGON Apprentice - first of the INNER RINGS.mp4`. **Direct page frames inspected:** `dc_research_002_frames/t31.jpg` (p.61), `t32.jpg` or `t33.jpg` (p.62), and `t34.jpg` (p.63).

## Page 61 — meditation opens; initial flight
- The separate preceding **Basic Personal Protection** section ends above the heading *Dragon Ride Meditation* on p.61.
- Standard meditation opening from **p.7** is referenced; opening itself not repeated here.
- Student crosses the **stone-paved courtyard of an ancient castle** in **rosy-pink dawn light**.
- Student climbs **stone steps built into the outer castle wall**.
- Waiting above is the student's **co-magician dragon**, wings **slightly spread**, **green-and-gold scales** glittering in new sun rays.
- Dragon bends **one knee**; student grasps **harness strap** to sit on dragon's **shoulders between his wings**.
- Dragon invites a flight around the world. Once seated, student and dragon are surrounded by an **invisible warm-air bubble**, described as protection against cold/airless skies and a **safety net in case student falls**. The text does not say that falling is physically impossible.
- Dragon launches with powerful wingbeat from the **castle wall**, wings catching **thermal air currents**.
- Nearby scenery: **patchwork fields**, dirt road lined by **trees**, canal bank lined by **low shrubbery**, canal using water from a **river beyond castle**.
- Dragon banks **left**, levels off and flies **southeast**. Journey over the western US: **Pacific Northwest** toward **Arizona and New Mexico**; pine-covered mountains yield to high desert plateaus, stacked red rock, ancient **cliff dwellings**, depths and twists of **Grand Canyon**.

## Page 62 — requests, further sightseeing, and a test
- Apprentice requests **Mississippi River, Atlantic Ocean, and ancient places in the Far East**. These are **requests**, not a claim that the text narrates the Atlantic crossing.
- Dragon promises requested destinations if student agrees to answer **one question at end**. Correct answer grants **right to move to studies of next Ring**; if failed, **Council of Elders** decides whether to repeat Apprentice studies or learn on **mundane levels only**.
- Student accepts. Dragon rides an **air stream along Mississippi River from Gulf of Mexico to Great Lakes** with outstretched wings.
- Student first perceives a flickering **electrical-system-like network** across the ground: straight stretches and lines routed around apparently damaged parts. Dragon then instructs **inner psychic eyes**; student sees imagined **Earth energy lines pulsing**, places like volcanoes glowing where lines reach surface. These are visualization claims, not measured geophysical phenomena.
- Dragon approaches **Great Wall of China**, shields student and dragon **from human sight**, flies low over ancient wall; student senses history of blood and war in stone as described in meditation.
- Dragon circles in heated air through **surreal clouds** and invites other destinations; student chooses, explores freely from dragon's-eye perspective, then returns when ready to starting castle.
- Dragon poses question that determines whether student may pass through the **Gate into Enchanter Ring**: **what tools does the student need to have power to cast a spell?**
- Narration instructs student to answer the dragon; **a lighted door opens in the castle wall**. Closing meditation from **p.8** is referenced. **End of meditation is on p.62.**

## Page 63 — answer supplied by author, outside meditation
- First lines of p.63 **after** the end-of-meditation notice on p.62: **only student and dragon know if answer was correct**. Conway's stated answer is **power comes from within; tools have no intrinsic power**.
- The new independent section **Dragon Alchemy** begins immediately afterward on p.63.
- Do **not** mistake the p.63 explanation as a narrated action during the flight, nor conflate the subsequent Enchanter transition ritual pp.65–66 with this meditation.

## Map and visual constraints
- Green-and-gold dragon; shoulder-seated rider **between wings** attached by grasping a **harness strap**; **invisible**, not coloured or solid, warm-air bubble. Morning setting rosy-pink. Stone stairs in outer wall. A lighted door appears near the conclusion.
- **No exact castle coordinates**, flight altitude, dragon size, specified harness design or precise geography of air currents is given. Southeast is an explicit heading on p.61, but the route is not fully geocoded.
- The p.58 **castle without walls** and the p.61 castle with outer wall are **separate meditation descriptions**. Do not presume identity or silently repair their difference.
- Request to see Atlantic Ocean is explicit, but no individually narrated Atlantic passage or sight details; leave unknown.
- Treat internal sensing of Earth energy lines as **the author's meditation imagery**, not independently established measurements.
- Standard opening p.7 and ending p.8 are **cross-references requiring their own source reading**.
