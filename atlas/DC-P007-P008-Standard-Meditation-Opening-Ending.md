- **p.7 reusable beginning** starts explicitly at **“(Beginning of meditation)”** and ends explicitly at **“(End of beginning)”** after leaving the well.

## Printed p.7: preparatory advice outside shared opening

1. Silence **phone and doorbell**; prevent interruptions by pets and other people. If needed, hang **do not disturb** sign.
2. Soft **instrumental** background music may block small irritating sounds.
3. Candles/incense are **not required**. If used, keep away from flammable items, ensure they cannot tip over, and avoid choking room with fumes.
4. Conway advises **against meditating lying down**, saying one might fall asleep; prefer a comfortable **chair, sitting upright, feet on floor**.
5. Conway characterizes deep practice as **astral travel or shamanic trance**, makes personal claims about time and awareness, and says the participant can **open their eyes and return any time** if uneasy. These are **author claims/advice**, not verified psychophysical facts or promises of safety.

## Printed p.7: explicitly marked reusable “Beginning of meditation”

Detailed source-ordered steps:

1. **Sit in chair**, **back straight**, **feet flat on floor**, **hands in lap**.
2. Take **three slow, deep breaths**, relaxing more muscles each time.
3. **Visualize brilliant white light** surrounding yourself, with **guardian spirits at your side**. Note: the specific source says **guardian spirits**, not “guardian dragon(s)” in this opening.
4. Let the body relax progressively until feeling **light and very comfortable**.
5. Imagine standing **beside a well**.
6. If **anything or anyone** in your life upsets you, **drop it/them into the well**. They fall down into **darkness away from you**.
7. **Walk away from the well**, leaving annoying people/things behind.
8. The source explicitly prints **“(End of beginning)”** after this movement. **No guarded door, dragon, or registry is in the reusable opening.**

**Visual constraints:** No source-specified material, shape, size, masonry or surroundings for the **well**; no specified visual form for guardian spirits; brilliance of white light is explicit. All figures/images remain part of Conway's visualization, not claimed actual objects.

## Between blocks, printed p.7 and continued p.8: particular first-entry meditation narrative (NOT shared beginning/ending)

**Printed p.7**:
1. Student feels they are **moving so quickly it seems they are not moving at all**.
2. **Velvety darkness** gives way to **bright sunlight** as a **huge wooden door** bound with **iron scrollwork and hinges** appears.
3. Student pulls a **heavy chain attached to a bell**; a deep reverberating sound follows; door opens slowly.
4. A **brightly coloured dragon** is just **inside** the doorway, asking why the student came.
5. On answer that they desire to study within the **Five Inner Rings**, dragon beckons them in; **door shuts behind**.
6. Student senses a **circular shape of the First Ring**, yet sees a **spacious, narrow room with slightly curved walls**. Sunlight streams through **skylights**, but **the ceiling cannot be seen**.

**Printed p.8**:
7. Looking **right and left**, student sees **endless lines of tables** displaying objects. Along walls: **shelves filled with scrolls/books**, **diagrams and maps**, and **open cabinets containing jars of dried herbs and tall bottles of oils**.
8. The dragon asks whether student desires to study with dragons and asks the student to **sign the registry if certain**.
9. A **large clawed foot** indicates a **table next to the closed door**. On it: **quill pen in an ink bottle** and an **open book**.
10. Student signs their own **name beneath earlier names** in the registry/book if intention to study is sincere.
11. The Gate guardian fastens a **tiny silver dragon figure to their clothing**.
12. Student is allowed to **ask guardian one question**.
13. Guardian leads them to a **bright cone of light** extending **from stone floor upward endlessly**.
14. Guardian says that on future visits student should identify as a student of the **Five Inner Rings**, and expresses hope of seeing them soon.
15. Guardian's **clawed hand** points toward brilliant cone; student walks toward it. **Only then does the labelled general ending start.**

**Visual fidelity prohibitions:** No named identity for Gate dragon, exact scale colours, dimensions/material for silver dragon ornament, maps' content, object inventory on endless tables, structural roof, dragon clothing or specific room compass position given. Do not construct the room as a large round hall: source says student **senses** a circular ring while visually seeing a **spacious but narrow room** with **slightly curved walls**. The Gate dragon is **brightly colored**, not a particular hue.

## Printed p.8: explicitly marked reusable “Ending”

1. **Walk into the light**. Student finds themself **back inside physical body**.
2. Slowly **move fingers and feet** until feeling comfortable again on the **physical plane**.
3. **The meditation is ended.**
4. The text explicitly marks **“(End of meditation)”**.

**Boundary question:** The starting “walk into the light” refers directly to the bright light cone just presented in the introductory meditation. Later meditations that cross-reference this shared ending use the printed wording, but **do not presume their scenes include the original Gate dragon, registry, door or cone's precise architecture** unless their own pages specify such elements.
