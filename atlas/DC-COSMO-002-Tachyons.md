# DC-COSMO-002 — Tachyons in Conway's Otherworld model

**Status:** LOCATED in archived visual-study notes; detailed printed-page wording NOT freshly verified; USER APPROVAL PENDING.
**Primary source:** D. J. Conway, *Mystical Dragon Magick*, “Understanding the Otherworld and the Gates”, particularly p. 38; context pp. 37–40.
**Evidence reused:** *Williams_Dragon_Study_Record_2026-10-10.md*, section “Otherworld, electrons and tachyons”. This is an earlier thematic visual reading, NOT a verbatim transcription.

## Source-supported account
- Conway invokes **hypothetical tachyons** alongside **electrons** in her account of overlapping Otherworld and Earth plane.
- She discusses paired/mirrored fields or universes, speed, energy, time, and particles appearing or disappearing.
- Page 40 connects the broader account with nervous-system processes, thought, movement, creativity and exchanges between universes.
- The archived summary does **not** document the exact printed claims distinguishing tachyons from electrons. Do not attribute a precise transport or signaling role without checking the original passage.

## Evidence boundaries
**Source claim:** Conway's cosmological hypothesis as presented in the book, not a validated scientific mechanism.
**Scientific context:** Tachyons are hypothetical faster-than-light particles in some theoretical discussions; no confirmed experimental observation establishes their existence.
**Unresolved:** Exact wording on p. 38; whether any particular particle direction, velocity or role is explicitly assigned by Conway; exact relationship to gateways and five-level diagram.
