# DC-P019 — Principles and Laws of Magick

**Source:** D. J. Conway, *Mystical Dragon Magick*, Apprentice material pp. 19–24, related discussion pp. 25–26.
**Evidence reused:** *Williams_Dragon_Study_Record_2026-10-10.md*, section “Apprentice material: principles, ethics and responsibility”.
**Status:** LOCATED in prior visual-study thematic summary; exact heading and complete printed enumeration not independently verified; USER APPROVAL PENDING.

## Archived principles (thematic paraphrases, NOT verbatim or original numbered laws)
- Self-knowledge and willingness to change
- Free will and respect for others' choices
- Humility concerning knowledge and abilities
- Responsibility for intention, action and consequences
- Clear purpose and sustained concentration
- Consent before doing magick, including healing, for another person
- Grounding and setting limits when concluding magickal work
- Acting according to ethics and judgment
- Compassion, service and a meaningful life
- Cumulative value of ordinary people's work

The earlier study identifies **Sairys** in a footnote as the dragon of Air and the East. The p.23 marked passage emphasizes the combined work of ordinary people over a single famous personality; p.24 markings highlight ethical rejection of unsuitable advice, compassion, humility and responsibility. Marginal marks are another reader's, not William's.

## Related pp. 25–26
Conway discusses white, black and gray practitioner categories and identifies her own approach with the third category (author's classification, not universal). The Rule of Three is introduced on p.26 as Conway's teaching on the return of directed magical power. See [Dragon Ethics](DC-P026-Dragon-Ethics.md).
