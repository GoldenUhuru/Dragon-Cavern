# DC-P064 — Testing Your Energy Balance

**Primary source:** D. J. Conway, *Mystical Dragon Magick*, *The Way of the Dragon Apprentice*, printed pp.64–65.

## P.64 — purpose and setup
1. **Purpose as stated:** The exercise is intended to help the **student, guardian dragon, and co-magician dragons** understand where the student's strengths and weaknesses in energy may lie, and help establish an **unbending trust with dragons**. Do not omit the guardian: the guardian is mentioned explicitly in the stated purpose, even though the active guide in the scene is the co-magician.
2. **Preparation:** Find a **quiet place**, the same type used for meditation. Surround yourself in visualized **white light** and **relax the body**. **No requirement to use the formal meditation opening or closing on pp.7–8 is stated in these paragraphs.**
3. **Setting/structure:** Visualize a **wide, very long board** balanced **on top of a golden pyramid**. The book calls this pyramid support the **fulcrum**. The board and fulcrum occupy a setting where **their glow is the only light**.
4. **Light gradation:** One end of this area is **brilliant white**, becoming **bright gray** as it nears the fulcrum. From the **central point** the light dims further until **only darkness** remains at the far opposite end. Neither compass direction nor a material/texture for the board is stated. Do **not** turn this into a fixed black-vs-white striped checkerboard, an indoor room, or a stand-alone golden pyramid building.
5. **Student's position:** The **co-magician dragon motions** for the student to stand on the board **directly over the pyramid support**.
6. **Board response:** Student becomes aware that **each physical movement** alters balance one way or another; gradually becomes aware that **thoughts** also influence the board's balance **within this mental challenge**.
7. **Telepathic explanation:** The dragon explains telepathically that the student is at the **calm pivotal point of Multiversal energy**. The student feels **safe and comfortable** at the centre. The **bright end** attracts through a sense of **absolute calm**, but its power feels **less** than at the center. The **dark end** offers a sense/allure of **deep power**, yet that, too, feels **less powerful** than the centre.

## P.65 — tests at each end, return and interpretation
8. **Move slightly toward dark/negative end:** Dragon instructs student to move **slightly toward dark end**. Student feels tempted to take up its dark energy but recalls lessons on **positive and negative energies**. Dragon motions with a hand to **slide gently**, reassuring student of safety. Student takes a **deep breath** and **by willpower** slides toward the **negative polarity**. Student relaxes on sensing the dragon's **astral hold**. Travels **only a short way** and stops.
9. **Observe without inventing sensations:** Student carefully notes **all feelings** associated with the negative energy and records any **happenings** (if any), for reflection on whether energy is currently out of balance. **The passage prescribes observation, not specific compulsory sensations, colours, visions or physiological responses.**
10. **Return to centre and pause:** Student returns to the **pivotal point**, relaxes **for a few moments**, then approaches the opposite end.
11. **Move slightly toward bright/positive end:** Student slides toward **bright, positive** end, under the dragon's **hold**, which prevents travelling too far into its tempting brilliance; this end offers a **different power temptation**. Again student notes feelings and any happenings. **Do not treat the bright end as automatically safest or strongest; the exercise privileges balance at the centre.**
12. **Finish:** Student returns to **centre**, then **steps down from board**.
13. **Reflection:** Ask whether both extremes felt uncomfortable, whether balance felt best **at the centre or slightly to either side**, and what all feelings/happenings meant personally. The co-magician dragon then explains **how to rebalance if needed**, with an **individualized** interpretation (the text does not specify a universal diagnosis).
14. **Privacy:** Conway says **only the student and their dragons** will know the exercise's outcome.
15. **Section ends:** The next printed heading on p.65 is **Dragon Apprentice Ritual**, a separate section.

## Evidence distinctions and visual constraints
- **The pyramid serves as the fulcrum beneath the board.** Board rests *on top of* pyramid, not inside a pyramid or suspended independently.
- The text explicitly describes a **gradient** from white through bright gray near centre to complete darkness at the other end; the text does **not** state that the board's material is white/black, or specify board/pyramid dimensions or a physical landscape beyond their light.
- **Guardian dragon and co-magician dragons** are included in the exercise's stated goal; **co-magician dragon** is the guide in the imagined scene. Do not invent its scale colour, size, facial details, anatomy, clothing, or number.
- **Thoughts and movements** affecting the balance are narrated as the participant's **visualization**, not an experimentally established psychophysical force.
- **Dark/negative first**, then **bright/positive**, with **return to centre** between, and a final **return to centre and step down**. This order is explicit and important for a faithful interactive adaptation.
- Dragon's **hand motion**, **astral holding**, **deep breath**, **willpower**, **short movement**, **relaxation interval**, and **personal observations** are explicit procedural details.
- **Bright end = alluring absolute calm**; **dark end = alluring deep power**; **both feel less powerful than center**. No automatic moral equation of bright=good/dark=evil is supported.
- Exact location within the Otherworld is **not specified**. No named gate or destination; no physical measurements, results scale, laboratory instruments, required crystals or specific duration.
- Do not confuse the new **Dragon Apprentice Ritual** beginning later on p.65 with this exercise.
- Any eventual illustrative or game interpretation must distinguish Conway's **textually specified elements** from **artist-designed details**.
