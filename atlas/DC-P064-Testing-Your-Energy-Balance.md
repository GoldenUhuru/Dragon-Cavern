# DC-P064 — Testing Your Energy Balance

**Primary evidence:** Conway, *Mystical Dragon Magick*, pp.64–65, directly read from supplied video around 00:36–00:37. **Status: VERIFIED from readable page frames; user approval PENDING.** A mental visualization exercise; no objective energy measurement is established.

## Page 64
- Purpose stated: explore perceived strengths and weaknesses of energy and build trust with guardian/co-magician dragons.
- Find quiet meditation space, surround self with **white light**, relax.
- Visualize **very long wide board balanced atop a golden pyramid**; board/pivot glow in otherwise unlit area.
- One end of scene **brilliant white**, fades to **bright gray** near fulcrum; beyond centre progressively dims into **darkness** at opposite end.
- Co-magician dragon indicates student should stand directly above pyramid support; physical movements and thoughts influence imagined board's balance.
- Dragon telepathically explains **calm pivotal point of Multiversal energy**; both bright and dark ends allure but feel less powerful than centre.

## Page 65
- Dragon directs slight slide toward dark end, while holding student safely; note feelings and experiences.
- Return to centre; slide a little toward bright end, similarly noting experiences.
- Return to centre and step down; reflect whether balance felt best in centre or to one side. Co-magician dragon offers an explanation for rebalancing if needed; outcome private to student and dragons.
- Section ends before **Dragon Apprentice Ritual** heading on p.65.

## Visual limits
The **golden pyramid is a support/fulcrum**, not a pyramid-shaped room or building. Do not assume compass orientation, material of board, dimensions, dragon appearance, or a measurable physical energy field.
