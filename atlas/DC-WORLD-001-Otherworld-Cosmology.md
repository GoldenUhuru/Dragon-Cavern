## Otherworld narrative (pp. 37–41)
Conway presents the Otherworld as sharing space with Earth but differing in vibration and time. Her discussion of electrons, tachyons, mirrored paired universes and physical effects is **the author's cosmological explanation**, not established scientific fact. Page 40 says most gateways to Dragon World and Faery Land are in Middleworld. The familiar Upperworld, Middleworld and Lowerworld are expanded by Highest and Underworld in Conway's model.
