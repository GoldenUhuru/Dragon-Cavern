## Page 59
- Some other apprentices are **humans with blue ribbons** and members of Dragon Apprentice Ring.
- Guardian dragon appears, nudges student to mingle and introduce self to several larger **co-magician dragons**; student laughs at elbow-nudge, takes guardian's hand, bows politely.
- Older dragons lower heads to meet student's gaze; eyes vary in colour and shape; dragons have **eyelashes**; scales and long tails described.
- Apprentices from Earth and other worlds/levels, some nonhumanlike, communicate telepathically.
- Low humming is telepathic speech of larger dragons, recalled from acceptance ritual.
- Each co-magician places hands either side of their apprentice's head and asks them to listen. **Music of the Multiverse** heard; a fleeting discordant note represents a world out of harmony.
- Tables with **juice and cakes**; student returns to introductions; co-magician dragons have individual personalities.

## Page 60 (meditation ending)
- Co-magicians form a long line with their personal students before them.
- Dragons welcome apprentices together; apprentices respond respectfully and pledge to strive to be worthy.
- Dragons hum; **bright doorways** appear around apprentices; student enters nearby portal.
- Ends with the standard meditation closing on **p.8**.
