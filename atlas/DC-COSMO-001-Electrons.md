# DC-COSMO-001 — Electrons in Conway's Otherworld model

**Status:** LOCATED in archived visual-study notes; detailed printed-page wording NOT freshly verified; USER APPROVAL PENDING.
**Primary source:** D. J. Conway, *Mystical Dragon Magick*, “Understanding the Otherworld and the Gates”, particularly p. 38; context pp. 37–40.
**Evidence reused:** *Williams_Dragon_Study_Record_2026-10-10.md*, section “Otherworld, electrons and tachyons”. This is an earlier thematic visual reading, NOT a verbatim transcription.

## Source-supported account
- Conway describes the Otherworld as overlapping the Earth plane while differing in vibration and the experience of time.
- On p. 38, she invokes **electrons** alongside hypothetical **tachyons** in an explanation of overlapping worlds.
- Her account discusses paired fields/universes mirroring and sustaining each other, speed, energy, time and particles appearing/disappearing.
- Page 40 develops the account toward nervous-system activity, thinking, movement, creativity and exchange between universes.
- The archive does **not** preserve enough exact printed wording to assign a specific individual mechanism to electrons as distinct from tachyons. Do not invent one.

## Evidence boundaries
**Source claim:** Conway's cosmological account, not independently demonstrated physical mechanism.
**Scientific context:** Electrons are experimentally established elementary particles. Their existence does not validate the proposed Otherworld mechanism.
**Unresolved:** Exact sentence-level explanation of electron behavior on p. 38; how Conway differentiates electrons from tachyons; any precise directions of exchange or game-world mechanics.
