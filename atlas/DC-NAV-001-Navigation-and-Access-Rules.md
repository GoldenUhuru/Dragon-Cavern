# DC-NAV-001 — Navigation and Access Rules

**Status:** Working source-linked synthesis, **awaiting user approval**. Consolidates earlier user-approved findings without reinterpreting them as universal laws.

## Map interpretation (p. 39)
Connections are **unarrowed lines**, not evidence of bidirectional travel, distance, travel time or open access.

## Gate distribution (p. 42)
All Otherworld levels **except Highest and Underworld** have Gates leading to side areas or other levels. Most are stationary; a few are **wandering**, and the source does not regard wandering Gates as safe to enter. Do not assign an individual Gate to wandering category unless its passage explicitly does.

## Named restrictions
- **Travelers' Gate** (Upperworld, p.42): reaches any Otherworld level **except Highest and Underworld**. Its spiral design is a marking, not an architectural specification.
- **Void Gate** (Upperworld, p.42): for Chaos Dragons only. Contact by Chaos Dragons does not establish human permission to pass.
- **Journey Gate** (Lowerworld, p.47): wandering; limited to Dragon Warriors, Dragon Mystics and dragons. Not the Travelers' Gate.
- **Four Hidden Gates of Wisdom** (pp.45,55–57): require each guardian's permission; prior Atlas also records initiation-stage restrictions. These rules are not superseded by a drawn line in the diagram.
- **Other-world deity shrines** (p.42): not open to Earth people, unlike shrines for ancient Earth pantheons. Shrines are sites, not necessarily Gates.

## Visual/game design rules
A gate's decoration ≠ its complete form. A line on a diagram ≠ an unlocked path. 'Near' ≠ 'inside'. Keep world, gate, side area, destination, guardian and eligibility as **separate nodes and typed relations**.

## Research gaps
Precise paths, architecture, number of intermediate steps, access rules of unreviewed gates, and p. 39 map coordinates remain unresolved. No claim is made that all Gates have been exhaustively catalogued.
