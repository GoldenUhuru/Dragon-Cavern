**Source:** D. J. Conway, *Mystical Dragon Magick*, printed pp.52–54.
**Evidence:** Directly inspected legible frames of user-supplied video `CH1 P42-66 _ The Way Of The DRAGON Apprentice - first of the INNER RINGS.mp4`: `dc_research_002_frames/t14.jpg` (p.52), `t16.jpg` / `t17.jpg` (p.53), `t18.jpg` (p.54).

## Critical document structure
Page 52 has heading **“Ritual for Attracting a Co-Magician Dragon”**, preceded by a glossary/list of dragon terms (not part of ritual). The ritual prints an explicitly bracketed **“Beginning of ritual”** block near the middle of p.52, followed by **“End of beginning”**. On p.54 an expressly marked **“Ending of ritual follows”** precedes the ending block. Between the blocks is a lengthy **ritual-specific body** concerning the Apprentice ribbon, crystals and attracting a co-magician.

A later **Dragon Apprentice Ritual** on p.65 directs the reader to the **beginning of ritual on p.52** and to the **ending of ritual on p.54**. This is evidence of borrowing the **marked opening and closing blocks**, **not evidence that the entire intervening co-magician rite must be copied into the Enchanter transition ritual**.

## Printed p.52 — co-magician ritual's own contextual ingredients
- Altar cloth, bowl **or geode** of **sea salt** and a bowl of **water**.
- **Seven-pointed star** (may be drawn on paper).
- Pieces of **amber, amethyst, aventurine and moss agate**.
- Preferable day **Sunday (Sun) or Monday (Moon)**.
- The author tells the reader to address the personal teacher respectfully as **Mon-Tah** until told another name. This belongs to co-magician teaching context.
**Boundary:** These items and preferred days belong to the p.52 co-magician ritual and **must not automatically be inserted into p.65's independently printed materials list** (which prefers Full Moon and jewelry/belt buckle instead).

### p.52 — explicitly marked common “Beginning of ritual” block
1. Spread the altar cloth on **floor or ground**, with **one side facing north**.
2. Place **salt bowl on left/west** side of cloth and **water bowl on right/east** side. Place ritual **stones on cloth before the practitioner's seat**.
3. **Kneel facing north**. Hold **left hand over salt** and **right hand over water** at same time; verbally call upon positive universal powers to charge the salt and water.
4. Pick up bowl of salt, **stand**, move **clockwise around altar cloth**, sprinkling a few grains at directional points **west, north, east, south** (that explicit sequence), return salt to cloth.
5. Pick up water bowl and **repeat actions performed with salt**.
6. End of marked beginning is explicitly labelled **“End of beginning.”**
**Precise caveat:** The opening is written around an altar with stones in the co-magician rite, but the later p.65 ritual has its own materials; cross-reference to *beginning* does **not** establish that p.65 requires the p.52 seven-pointed star, specific gems, or the p.52 Sunday/Monday timing.

## p.52–p.54 — intervening **co-magician ritual-specific content**, not shared opening/closing
- Immediately **after** “End of beginning” on p.52: blue Apprentice ribbon draped across shoulders while kneeling or sitting cross-legged facing north. This is **not** inside the marked opening block.
- On p.53: a **quartz point** is used and placed in cloth's center with its point **north**; **amethyst on northern edge**, **amber on eastern edge**, **aventurine on southern edge**, **moss agate on western edge**, each accompanied by its own dedication as Conway describes. The exact named stone, directional association and order should remain source-labelled, not silently treated as universal altar rules.
- The practitioner breathes evenly and feels presence of **personal guardian**. On p.54 they focus on qualities desired in a **new co-magician**, not its imagined physical looks. The co-magician may approach from behind; guardian dragons may have mottled colours, but Conway says the new co-magician has **one specific colour**; its name may be withheld at first meeting. Greeting/learning follows.
- On p.54 before marked ending: **after co-magician encounter**, practitioner opens eyes; holds **both palms on altar cloth** and thanks dragons, guardian and the **new co-magician**, and releases surplus power into Earth. This wording is **tailored to the co-magician rite**, and **precedes** the marked ending. Do not import these ritual-specific thanks to p.65 verbatim.

## p.54 — expressly marked “Ending of ritual follows”
1. **Pour the water** into a **potted plant** or **onto the ground**.
2. Conway claims the water has healing energy with vibrations compatible with Earth: record as her spiritual rationale, **not an empirical fact**.
3. **Put away the rest of the ritual items** until next needed.
4. A new section heading **“Dragons of the Elements”** follows and does not belong to closing.
**No printed instruction in the marked ending to sprinkle additional salt, speak another invocation, or use the p.53 gem layout.** Do not invent.
