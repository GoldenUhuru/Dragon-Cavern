## Page 65
- Listed ingredients: **altar cloth; small bowls of sea salt and water; altar stones; selected piece of jewelry or belt buckle** placed on altar cloth.
- **Preferable day: Full Moon**.
- Use standard ritual/ceremony opening **p.52**.
- Visualize **solid walls of a great cave** forming around student, **large flight of dragons** greeting with humming, warmth of their bodies, rustling/folding wings.
- Student asks to pass into **Enchanter Ring**, saying they have honoured Apprentice promise.
- **Aldram, Keeper of the Book**, appears as ancient dragon who took original Apprentice oath; student extends both hands palms up, Aldram lays hands over them.
- Aldram asks whether student takes oath to learn the ways of the Enchanter of the Five Inner Rings.

## Page 66

- Take jewelry into **power hand**, close fist and raise over head; touch jewelry to **centre of forehead**, then **heart**.
- Student accepts Enchanter Ring studies and agrees not to disclose Enchanter arts to those outside that Ring.
- Sit quietly for a few minutes; **use ending of ritual on p.54**.
