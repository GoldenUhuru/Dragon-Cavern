**Source:** D. J. Conway, *Mystical Dragon Magick*, printed pp.65–66, section “Dragon Apprentice Ritual”.

## Critical contextual distinction
Although the *heading* says “Dragon Apprentice Ritual,” the ceremony is about an **Apprentice seeking admission into the Enchanter Ring**, the second of the Five Inner Rings. The candidate says they have remained true to their earlier Apprentice promise. Do **not** describe this as the first Apprentice admission ritual, or conflate it with the separate Acceptance in Dragon Studies Meditation (pp.30–33).

## Printed page 65 — preparation and oath invitation

### Ingredients and recommended timing
1. **Altar cloth**.
2. **Small bowls:** one of **sea salt** and another of **water**.
3. **Your altar stones** (possessive present in source).
4. **Chosen piece of jewelry**, or **even a belt buckle**, placed **on the altar cloth before you**.
5. **Preferable day: Full Moon** — explicitly preferable rather than an exclusively mandatory day.
6. Use the **ritual/ceremony opening on p.52** (cross-reference; full opening not reproduced here).

### The beginning of the scripted ritual
7. **Close eyes and relax body**.
8. **Solid walls of a great cave** materialize around the student.
9. **A large flight of dragons** greets student through audible **humming**. Student feels **warmth of their bodies** and hears **rustling of folding wings**. Do not specify dragon number, colours or cave architecture absent from page.
10. **Open eyes and speak a greeting to all dragon-kind**; request **passage into Enchanter Ring** and state that the student's earlier promise as an Apprentice has been honoured.
11. Student **senses the presence of Aldram**, **Keeper of the Book**, an **ancient dragon**. **Crucial correction to previous entry:** source says Aldram **took/received the STUDENT'S apprenticeship oath when the student began studying**, NOT that Aldram himself took an apprenticeship oath. Earlier phrasing “ancient dragon who took original Apprentice oath” was ambiguous and has been replaced.
12. Student holds **both hands, palms up**; Aldram lays his hands over theirs; student feels **light touch of dragon skin**.
13. **Aldram asks whether student takes oath to learn ways of the Enchanter of the Five Inner Rings.** Page 65 ends after this question.

## Printed page 66 — response, consecratory gesture, pledge and closing

14. Student responds to Aldram with a **rhyming spoken oath**. Content commitments: learn Enchanter ways and spellcraft; employ power to **help rather than harm the Multiverse**; oppose malice and spiteful curses; learn for benefit of all; call dragons for assistance. The source includes imagery of **leaf, blossom, bright candle and night**. These are textually attested *words/images in a recitation*, **not instructions to put leaves, blossoms or an extra candle on altar**. The precise full oath is not reproduced here; reread printed p.66 if exact recital is needed.
15. Take the chosen **jewelry item** in **power hand**, explicitly defined as **the hand used most**.
16. **Tighten fist around jewelry** as much as possible, while **holding that hand overhead**.
17. A brief, separate affirmative exclamation on p.66: **“Every hour is dragon power!”**. Do not silently omit its place between the raised fist and the following touching gesture.
18. **Touch jewelry to centre of forehead, then over heart**. The source does not mention throat or third-eye markings or prescribe additional body points.
19. Student states acceptance of **studies of Enchanter Ring**, undertaking to do their best, and promises **not to speak of Enchanter arts to those not of that Ring**.
20. **Sit quietly for a few minutes**.
21. **Use ending of ritual on p.54** (separate cross-reference not quoted here).
22. The printed passage explicitly marks **“(Ending of the ritual)”** on **p.66**. The ritual does **not** continue beyond p.66.

## Source-faithful visual and gameplay constraints
- **Great cave** is imagined to form around participant; the only source-specified features on pp.65–66 are **solid cave walls**, **large gathering/flight of dragons**, **humming**, **body warmth**, **folding-wing sounds**, presence and **light skin contact** of **ancient Aldram**. Do not invent stalactites, altars within cave, dragon anatomy, clothes, thrones, dragon numbers or colour.
- The **physical altar** holds the small bowls, altar stones and chosen jewelry (or belt buckle). Do not assume cave imagery and physical objects are all manifested in the same plane as a literal spatial setting.
- **Aldram receives the student's original Apprentice oath**, is now present as Keeper of the Book and prompts the Enchanter oath. Distinguish him from any unnamed dragon taking an oath. Page provides **no new specific depiction of Aldram**; reuse only independently verified Atlas material if illustration is later desired.
- **The spoken oath's leaf/blossom/candle imagery is verbal**, not a materials checklist. The **jewelry or belt buckle** is an alternative selection: while the printed instructions subsequently use “jewelry,” do not silently replace the earlier belt-buckle option with a second required separate item.
- Do not equate the ceremony with automatic approval of advancement or invent an evaluative council result; the student **requests entry and pledges**.
- **Opening p.52** and **ending p.54** are explicit dependencies; they must be referenced or independently verified before a fully executable ritual representation. Only pp.65–66 were reexamined in this review.
- Ritual and metaphysical events are **Conway's guided spiritual practice**, not empirically verified physical phenomena.
