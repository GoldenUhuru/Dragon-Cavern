## Recorded sequence (paraphrase, not verbatim script)
1. The approach includes a hill, obstructing boulders and a cave.
2. **Mirraglas** guides the approach.
3. Crystal reflections and an altar fire create a challenge: discern the true fire among reflections.
4. The participant faces a choice about undertaking the Dragon path.
5. The meditation involves a register, dragon witnesses and **Alberon**, named **Keeper of the Book**.
6. The ceremony leads to the designation **Daughter or Son of the Dragon** within Conway's system.
7. A return concludes the experience.

The archived summary does not establish exact stage order beyond this broad outline, exact dialogue, physical layout, number or appearance of dragons, the register's design, or detailed return route. Do not invent those as book facts.
