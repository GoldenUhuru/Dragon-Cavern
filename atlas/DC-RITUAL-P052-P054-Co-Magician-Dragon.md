# DC-RITUAL-P052-P054 — Ritual for Attracting a Co-Magician Dragon

**Source:** D. J. Conway, *Mystical Dragon Magick*, printed pages 52–54.  
**Evidence:** V — directly checked against the uploaded source-video frames corresponding to pp. 52, 53 and 54.  
**Status:** Verified transcription-based summary; **awaiting user approval**.  
**Category:** Ritual procedure, not a fixed geographic place.

## Purpose and title
The ritual seeks a personal co-magician dragon, one of a specially trained clan working with students throughout the Multiverse. Address a personal dragon teacher as **Mon-Tah** until the dragon grants permission to use a personal name.

## Page 52: Ingredients and opening
- Ingredients listed: altar cloth; small bowl or geode of sea salt; bowl of water; seven-pointed star (may be drawn on paper); amber, amethyst, aventurine and moss agate.
- Preferable day: Sunday (Sun) or Monday (Moon).
- Place altar cloth on floor or ground, one side facing north.
- Salt bowl to left/west; water bowl to right/east; stones in front of the seated position.
- Kneel facing north; left hand over salt, right over water; speak invocation of positive universal powers.
- Walk clockwise sprinkling a few grains of salt at directional points **west, north, east, south**, then repeat with water.
- After opening: drape blue Apprentice ribbon across shoulders; kneel or sit cross-legged facing north; affirm safety within the Dragon Apprentice's ring.

## Page 53: Crystal and stone sequence
**Source discrepancy:** Quartz crystal is used in the ritual on p. 53, but not named in p. 52's ingredients list. Do not silently reconcile.

| Item | Placement | Stated function/intention |
|---|---|---|
| Quartz crystal | Centre of cloth, point facing north | Protection against negative energies, communication with dragons, attracting suitable dragon teacher |
| Amethyst | Northern edge | Spiritual/psychic wisdom, sensitivity, seeing through illusions to truth |
| Amber | Eastern edge | History, past lives, Akashic Records and applying knowledge |
| Aventurine | Southern edge | Adventure, unexpected knowledge, new Gates and worlds |
| Moss agate | Western edge | Thought/intent strength, discernment, healing and balance |

After moss agate, close eyes and breathe evenly until relaxed and sensing personal guardian dragon's presence.

## Page 54: Calling, encounter, closing
- Focus intent on obtaining a co-magician with complementary qualities, not on visualizing a particular appearance.
- Call mentally until sensing a larger dragon behind; exchange greetings and possibly names.
- Guardian/small dragons may be mottled; text says co-magician dragon has one specific colour. It may differ from expectations and may not offer a name on first meeting.
- Be friendly; book says another co-magician is unlikely to be assigned.
- On opening eyes, dragons may be glimpsed at peripheral vision, but fade if looked at directly.
- Palms on altar cloth; offer thanks to all dragons, guardian and co-magician; release altar power into Earth; wish harmlessness, love and healing to Multiverse.
- Pour water into a potted plant or onto ground; store other ritual items.

## Source-critical distinctions
The sequence is the **book's described ritual**, not independently established effects. A dragon appearing behind the practitioner in this procedure does not establish a universal geographic location. A ritual description is not automatically a game-world map feature.
