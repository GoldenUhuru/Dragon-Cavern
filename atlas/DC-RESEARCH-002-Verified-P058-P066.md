# DC-RESEARCH-002 — Direct source verification pp.58–66

**Primary source:** D. J. Conway, *Mystical Dragon Magick*, printed pp.58–66, reviewed directly from uploaded video `CH1 P42-66 _ The Way Of The DRAGON Apprentice - first of the INNER RINGS.mp4`, frames approximately 00:26–00:39. **Evidence: V (visually read). Approval: USER-APPROVED for all six sections, 2026-10-24.** These are descriptions of Conway's guided meditations, magical claims and rituals, not independently demonstrated physical facts. This record supersedes the six earlier heading-only placeholders.

## DC-P058 — Welcoming Other Dragons Meditation, pp.58–60
**p58:** Standard meditation opening p7; blue ribbon draped over shoulders, slow deep breathing; stone courtyard of a mighty **castle without walls**; dragons of all sizes and other apprentices.
**p59:** Some apprentices are human with blue ribbons; guardian appears, nudges student to mingle, and holds student's hand; older co-magician dragons lower heads for eye contact. Their eyes have varied colours/shapes and **eyelashes**. Nonhumanlike apprentices from other worlds/levels can communicate telepathically. Low hum of larger dragons' telepathic speech; co-magicians place hands either side of their apprentice's head, inviting them to hear **music of the Multiverse**, including a discordant tone associated with an out-of-harmony world. Tables with **juice and cakes**. Dragons have individual personalities.
**p60:** Co-magicians form line with their students; welcome exchanged; humming produces bright doorways, student enters nearby portal; standard meditation ending p8.
**Visual limits:** No fixed dragon count, courtyard geometry, colour of older dragons or portal design specified; do not turn courtyard into a walled enclosure.

## DC-P060 — Basic Personal Protection, pp.60–61
**p60:** Conway distinguishes perceived magical attacks from ill-wishing/jealousy/envy; recommends visualizing a globe of brilliant **white light**, or **shining blue armor** if near an antagonist; also a meaningful consecrated good-luck charm (e.g. crystal, possibly held by dragon figure). Charm ritual references frankincense smoke, heat above candle flame, water and earth as four elements. Author also stresses avoiding predictable conflict.
**p61:** If conflict unavoidable, **Look Elsewhere** technique: avoid eye contact, walk slowly and smoothly, mentally redirect attention away from self.
**Limits:** Magical efficacy and aura/vibration explanation are Conway's claims, not established science; candle heat carries ordinary burn risk.

## DC-P061 — Dragon Ride Meditation, pp.61–63
**p61:** Opening p7. Rosy dawn in **stone-paved courtyard of ancient castle**, stone stairs built into **outer wall**. Co-magician dragon with **green-and-gold scales**, wings slightly spread, bends knee; student climbs by **harness strap** to shoulders **between wings**. Invisible **warm-air bubble** protects both from cold, airless sky and falling. Launches from castle wall on thermals. Below: patchwork fields, dirt road, trees, canal, river. Banks **left**, flies **southeast** across western US: Pacific Northwest, desert plateaus, Arizona/New Mexico, red rocks, cliff dwellings, Grand Canyon.
**p62:** Student requests Mississippi, Atlantic and Far East. Dragon requires one answer at ride's end; successful answer permits next Ring, failure referred to **Council of Elders**. Travels Mississippi **Gulf of Mexico to Great Lakes**, imagines energy lines in Earth; visits **Great Wall of China** shielded from human sight; student can choose further places. Returns to castle; question asks what tools give power to cast a spell. Lighted door in castle wall; closing p8.
**p63:** Answer explicitly supplied: **power comes from within; tools have no power in themselves**.
**Visual limits:** Distinguish castle-with-outer-wall in this meditation from wall-less courtyard of p58; do not invent route via Otherworld.

## DC-P063 — Dragon Alchemy, pp.63–64
**p63:** Conway frames alchemy chiefly as transformation/balance of **body, mind and spirit**, with lead/gold metaphor. Claims symbolic teachings passed teacher to student and links dragons to other planets, **Atlantis, Lemuria, Emerald Tablet, Thoth**; describes fraudulent alchemists and historical chemical experiments.
**p64:** Magical/spiritual alchemy described as concealed in symbols; **planetary powers**, **element mixtures**, **Philosopher's Stone**, spiritual aim, theory before visualization.
**Limits:** Historical claims about dragons, Atlantis, Lemuria and transmission are attributed to Conway, not independently verified; no source-specific laboratory diagram.

## DC-P064 — Testing Your Energy Balance, pp.64–65
**p64:** Quiet meditative place, white-light visualization, relax. Imagine **wide, very long board balanced on top of a golden pyramid** in otherwise unlit space. One side **brilliant white**, fades to **bright gray** at fulcrum; other side darkens into **darkness**. Co-magician dragon directs student to stand at centre; movement/thought affect imagined board; centre described as calm pivot of Multiversal energy; both bright and dark ends exert allure.
**p65:** Dragon holds student safely while sliding a short distance toward dark end; student notes feelings, returns to centre, then explores bright end, returns and steps down. Reflect on preferred balance; dragon offers personalized interpretation.
**Limits:** Imagined symbolic balance, not a validated energy measurement; no compass orientation or board material given.

## DC-P065 — Dragon Apprentice Ritual, pp.65–66
**CRITICAL CORRECTION:** Despite heading, this is an **Apprentice-to-Enchanter transition ritual**, not initiation *into* the Apprentice Ring. **It explicitly ends on p66**; no missing continuation.
**p65:** Materials: altar cloth, small bowls of **sea salt** and **water**, altar stones, **jewelry or belt buckle**. Preferable day **Full Moon**. Ritual opening **p52**. Visualize solid walls of **great cave**, large flight of dragons humming, warmth, folding wings. Student requests entrance into **Enchanter Ring**. **Aldram, Keeper of the Book**, receives hands palms-up and asks whether student takes Enchanter oath.
**p66:** Rhymed oath to study Enchanter practices and help rather than harm; take jewelry in **power hand**, close fist, raise above head, touch **forehead** then **heart**; pledge to study and not disclose Enchanter arts outside that Ring; sit quietly; ritual closing **p54**; explicit printed **End of the ritual**.
**Limits:** Cave dimensions, dragon appearances and exact oath wording should not be invented; opening p52 and closing p54 require cross-reference.
