# DC-P057 — Gark-Yin

**Source:** D. J. Conway, *Mystical Dragon Magick*, printed p. 57.  
**Status:** VERIFIED from supplied source photograph; **awaiting user approval**.  
**Category:** Named dragon / guardian of one of the Four Hidden Gates.

## Identification and relationship
- **Name:** Gark-Yin.
- **Gender in source:** Female ('her', 'she').
- **Role:** Watches **the Gate to the Western Mystery School**, one of the Four Hidden Gates.
- **Appearance tradition:** Eastern dragon.
- **World/map relationship:** Gate → Western Mystery School. The four Hidden Gates are in the centre of the Five Inner Rings (pp. 55–56); do not infer that the Western School's Gate is physically at the west side of the centre, or invent architectural features.

## Exact descriptive details (p. 57)
- Azure-blue scales with gold edging.
- Red, thread-thin whiskers wave from above the eyes and around the nose.
- Long, slender body; four legs, each with four dark-gold claws. Legs small relative to body.
- No discernible division where neck joins body.
- Wide, blunt head.
- Long, feathery moth-like antennae arise from top of head.
- Antennae have a patterned combination of gold, azure blue and pure red; twist and turn in response to air vibrations, especially emotions and mental thoughts.
- **No wings.**
- Golden-brown eyes, watchful but compassionate.

## Evidence boundaries
- No specific Gate architecture is described in this passage.
- Western Mystery School is the destination name; not proof of a westward geographical placement.
- Do not add horns, wings, human traits or unmentioned anatomy in a source-faithful illustration.
