## Page 63
- Conway distinguishes alchemy as **spiritual transformation and balancing body, mind and spirit**, rather than simply changing lead into gold; lead/gold used as metaphor for refinement.
- She presents a narrative of older philosophical/sage traditions, symbols and double meanings, and secret teacher-to-student transmission.
- Conway claims dragons taught alchemy on other planets and later Earth, with teachings connected to **Atlantis, Lemuria, Emerald Tablet and Thoth**. These are her esoteric/historical assertions, not verified history.
- She describes fraudulent 'puffers' and chemical experimentation as an early precursor to later chemical practice.

## Page 64 (continuation)
- Her account describes the magical/spiritual side of alchemy becoming concealed and reduced to symbols.
- Explicit concepts: **planetary powers**, **mixtures of elements**, **Philosopher's Stone**.
- Spiritual alchemy is presented as highest aim; understanding how a principle works before applying it to spiritual visualization.
- Section ends before heading **Testing Your Energy Balance** on p.64.
