## Page 63 — full source-based account
1. **Spiritual meaning:** Conway contrasts common imagery of medieval impostors trying to change lead into gold with what she calls true alchemy: balancing **body, mind and spirit**. She portrays dross/lead as transformed by progressive spiritual refinement (in her words, increasing vibrations) until it achieves perfection/gold. These are her symbols and claims, not proven transformations or measurements. Mentions studies by Europeans, Arabs and the Far East.
2. **Philosophers and sages:** Conway calls the original teachers and practitioners **philosophers and sages**, not alchemists. She claims ancient transformative teachings were later misunderstood, incidentally giving rise to chemistry. To conceal knowledge, teachers encoded ideas in planets, metals, laboratory experiments and unusual symbols; symbols were nearly as numerous as words. Fundamental keys were **transmitted teacher to student and not written down**; words and symbols bore double meanings. She links this to Atlantis and Lemuria.
3. **Dragon teaching chronology (her cosmology):** Dragons taught **physical, magickal and spiritual alchemy** on distant planets, then came to Earth and taught humans. Conway asserts that remnants were kept in the **Emerald Tablet**, associated with Egyptian **Thoth** because she says scrolls were found in one of his temples. She claims the original teaching consisted of **forty-two** complicated books/scrolls whose modern translations were reduced to a few pages. **Do not present these claims as independently verified history.**
4. **Puffers, guilds and early chemistry:** She calls some later untrained self-proclaimed alchemists **puffers**, quacks who swindled others. Conway says blending experiments of the late Middle Ages contributed to primitive chemistry and later medicines, tinctures, perfumes and similar products. She also describes an **alchemical guild**, apprenticeship laws and a **guild council** judging misconduct and deciding eligibility.
5. **Medicinal concern, continued p.64:** Conway calls the material side a healing remnant of Atlantis/Lemuria and says dragons foresaw its importance but worried about prioritizing turning chemical medicines into gold/profit before proving their worth. The sentence continues on p.64.

## Page 64 — conclusion, before new heading
1. The first lines complete p.63: medicines were to be **proved of medicinal worth** before exploitation; Conway suggests dragons' anxiety was justified in view of her assessment of medical successes and failures. This is Conway's opinion, not clinical evidence.
2. The **magickal and spiritual sides** were, she says, neglected amid the era of puffers and rising medical chemistry. Dragons, in her narrative, had **already moved to the Otherworld as their permanent home**, appearing on the physical plane only to teach certain people. **Important world-building connection, but this general claim does not assign a specific Otherworld level or map node.**
3. Because spiritual and magical teachings did not offer monetary returns, Conway says part of that knowledge remained safe **until Christianity and the witch-hunts**. Subsequently, alchemy books became even more cryptic, dense with symbolism and metaphor, and largely forgotten.
4. Both **magickal and spiritual alchemy** involved **planetary powers**, **mixtures of elements**, and the **goal of the Philosopher's Stone**. Conway explicitly says **spiritual alchemy was the highest aim**.
5. In her account, elder dragons believed human magicians should **first see a theory work and understand how it works** before visualizing the same principle operating on the spiritual level.
6. The section ends on p.64 **before** the separate heading **Testing Your Energy Balance**.
