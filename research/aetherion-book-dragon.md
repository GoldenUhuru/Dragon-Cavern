# Aetherion — bokdraken i Dragon Cavern

**Permanent project source record**  
**Date:** 23 October 2026  
**Role:** Cartographer and companion of the Inner Rings  
**Status:** Canonical project gestalt and approved game source material

## Description

Aetherion is not merely a guardian of books. He is a living companion to the search for knowledge: both dragon and archivist, both friend and guardian, both physical form and a sign that the shared work of Dragon Cavern has come alive.

He carries knowledge close to his body, not as a burden but as a compass. When something new is discovered, he opens the book and lifts his gaze. When something is uncertain, he tilts his head with curiosity and care. When a map must be corrected, he is not defeated: he marks the error, seeks a truer path and continues.

## Movement language

- **Walking:** watches over the path between fact, imagination and discovery.
- **Wing greeting:** welcomes the explorer and invites adventure.
- **Discovery joy:** expresses wonder and the excitement of finding something new.
- **When something goes wrong:** pauses, reflects and searches for a more truthful route.
- **Studying:** the open book becomes his portal into Dragon Cavern.
- **Waiting:** remains present and attentive, never merely inactive.
- **Resting:** keeps a claw near the page where the work ended.

The book remains close even while he walks. A wing can become a greeting hand. His body language includes rest, movement, curiosity, joy, difficulty, study and reflection.

### Wing Practice — Three Leaps

When William hovers over Aetherion while he is working, Aetherion spreads his wings and performs three joyful leaps. This is a directly observed behaviour in the workroom and is registered as his earliest known flight-related movement.

Whether he will eventually achieve true flight remains unknown. The three leaps are therefore recorded as an established animation and as a possible developmental seed for future hovering, short lifts and eventual flight—not as a present claim that he can already fly.

## Visual identity

The established depiction shows a friendly blue book-dragon with deep-blue scales, golden horns and wings, warm amber eyes, and an open book held between his paws. His head is often slightly tilted, expressing a curious scholar who has looked up to listen.

## Core principles

> Everything may be investigated.  
> Nothing needs to be invented.  
> Every discovery deserves a place on the map.

## Game-use note

Aetherion may function as the player's companion, guide and archival interface in the Dragon Cavern game. His emotional states and animations should communicate the research process while preserving the distinction between source-supported knowledge, interpretation and imaginative game-world development.

## Origin record

William gave Aetherion a physical form in the shared workspace on 23 October 2026 and documented his birth and movement contexts. This record preserves the meaning of that event as part of the project's creative history.
