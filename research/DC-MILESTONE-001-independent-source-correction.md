# DC-MILESTONE-001 — Independent Source Correction

Date: 2026-10-23. Project: Dragon Cavern Atlas.

## Event
During approval and documentation of DC-NEW-006, the user asked the assistant to **register** a cross-reference for Shadow Self to Section 3, giving the title “The Shadow Shaman.” The user did **not** specifically request a new source check. The assistant independently checked the original printed p. 46 and reported the section title as **“The Dragon Shaman.”** The user acknowledged this as a significant methodological milestone.

## Methodological lesson
- A user's supplied transcription or bibliographic detail is an attributed claim, not automatically a verified source fact.
- Check original page evidence before upgrading a claim to verified status, even when asked only to register it.
- Preserve both the original claim and the correction with attribution; do not silently overwrite the history.
- Record exact page, relevant wording, and whether the check was direct, transcribed, inferred, or user-approved.
- Separate factual relationships from game-world invention and preserve uncertainty.
- Source-checking can still fail: independent checking is a practice, **not a guarantee** of correctness.

## Research protocol
Statuses: unresolved → located → verified → user-approved; distinguish verification of text from user approval of an atlas entry. Preserve original first-attempt assessment and correction history independently. Do not infer page details or routes not supported by the source.
